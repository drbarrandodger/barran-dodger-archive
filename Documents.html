What Phase 1 deliberately does not do

  • It does not normalise or rename documentary artefacts.
  • It does not treat repository filenames as validated legal or factual conclusions.
  • It does not publish a new broad browse view of potentially sensitive source material by default.
  • It does not implement forensic modules or full archive indexing yet.
