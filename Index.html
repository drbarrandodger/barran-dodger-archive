Evidence Classification

The archive distinguishes, where practicable, between documented facts, primary sources, official records, judicial findings, official determinations, corroborated material, the author's account, allegations, inference, interpretation, disputed material and unresolved research questions.

Inclusion of a person, organisation, allegation, event or legal concept does not by itself establish wrongdoing or legal liability. Readers are encouraged to inspect the underlying primary materials and consider relevant counter-evidence.
