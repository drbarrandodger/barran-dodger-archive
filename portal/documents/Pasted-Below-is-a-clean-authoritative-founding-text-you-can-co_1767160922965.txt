Barran Dodger Legal & Ethical Trust Fund

The Barran Dodger Legal & Ethical Trust Fund is a non-profit ethical trust established to convert sworn testimony, verified documentation, and lived evidence into public-benefit action.

Founding Basis

The Trust is founded upon the complete body of testimony, affidavits, evidence archives, and public records authored and compiled by Barran Dodger. These materials form the ethical and evidentiary foundation of the Trust’s mandate.

Purpose

The Trust exists for the benefit of humanity to:
	•	Uphold ethical governance and lawful accountability
	•	Protect vulnerable persons and whistleblowers
	•	Support truth-telling grounded in evidence and due process
	•	Advocate for justice, dignity, and institutional transparency
	•	Prevent abuse of power through ethical and legal education, advocacy, and public interest work

Nature of the Trust
	•	Non-profit and non-commercial
	•	Faith-neutral and non-partisan
	•	Operated solely for public benefit, not personal enrichment
	•	Governed by transparency, accountability, and ethical restraint

Role of the Founder

Barran Dodger serves as Founder and Living Witness, providing testimony and evidence that inform the Trust’s mission. The Founder does not claim special authority beyond documented experience, sworn statements, and ethical responsibility to the public.

Activities

The Trust may:
	•	Preserve and publish verified testimony and evidence
	•	Provide ethical and legal advocacy resources
	•	Engage with institutions, regulators, and the public
	•	Support education on human rights, administrative justice, and ethical governance
	•	Facilitate lawful pathways for reform, redress, and protection

Public Commitment

The Barran Dodger Legal & Ethical Trust Fund commits to:
	•	Truth over narrative
	•	Evidence over ideology
	•	Accountability over silence
	•	Dignity over harm

This Trust represents the ethical transformation of lived harm into public service, ensuring that documented truth serves justice, prevention, and the common good.
