# Design system

## Tone

Serious, archival, documentary, accessible, calm, and high-trust.

## Foundation tokens

- parchment background
- dark bronze / umber accent
- restrained warning red for caution states
- serif headings, plain sans body copy
- rounded but sober card surfaces

## Reusable presentation primitives

- `card` for archive/document/article summaries
- `status-badge` for evidence-state labelling
- `search-shell` for limited discovery interfaces
- `path-chip` for preserved source-path references

## Accessibility rule

Every status and interaction must be distinguishable by text, symbol, and structure—not only by colour.
