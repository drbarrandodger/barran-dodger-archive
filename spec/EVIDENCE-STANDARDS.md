# Evidence standards

Phase 1 defines shared status labels only. Applying them across the corpus is deferred.

## Status vocabulary

- **Documented fact** — directly supported by cited evidence without added interpretation
- **Primary-source record** — contemporaneous or direct-source artefact
- **Official record** — issued by an authority or institution
- **Judicial finding** — traceable to a court or tribunal finding
- **Official determination** — a formal decision or notice by an authorised body
- **Corroborated material** — supported by more than one evidentiary strand
- **Author's account** — first-person authored statement requiring attribution
- **Allegation** — asserted claim not framed as concluded fact
- **Inference** — reasoned conclusion derived from evidence
- **Interpretation** — analytical or editorial reading
- **Possible legal characterisation** — a tentative legal frame, not a final conclusion
- **Unverified material** — preserved but not yet verified
- **Disputed material** — known to be contested or contradicted
- **Research question** — open question retained for future work

## Standard of care

- Do not collapse allegation into fact.
- Do not collapse interpretation into source description.
- Do not mark legal conclusions as settled unless the source itself establishes them.
- Do not rely on colour alone; every status requires text and symbol-level differentiation.
