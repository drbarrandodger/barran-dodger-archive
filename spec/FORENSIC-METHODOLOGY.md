# Forensic methodology

This document reserves methodological boundaries for later forensic phases.

## Phase 1 stance

- preserve inputs
- define entities, relationships, and status labels
- avoid full forensic modules, scoring systems, or claim adjudication

## Future forensic requirements

Later phases may add chain-of-custody mapping, citation graphs, timeline correlation, duplicate resolution, provenance review, and issue-based dossiers, but only on top of preserved record identifiers.
