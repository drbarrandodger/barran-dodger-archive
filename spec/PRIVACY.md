# Privacy

## Phase 1 privacy posture

The repository contains filenames and materials that may signal personal, official, or sensitive content. Phase 1 therefore separates preservation from publication.

## Publication rules

- keep the pre-restructure inventory in `spec/` rather than the public shell
- do not auto-publish newly discovered sensitive material
- treat repository filenames as potentially sensitive metadata when deciding what to surface publicly
- prefer collection-level shells over record-level public browsing until review criteria are established
